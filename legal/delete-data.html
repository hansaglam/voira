How to Request Deletion

To request deletion, email ethemsincarbusiness@gmail.com with the subject line: Voira Data Deletion Request. Include the email address used for your Voira account so we can verify and process the request.

You may also use in-app options (where available) to clear local practice data on your device. Local clearing is not the same as a full account deletion request.

What Can Be Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will delete or de-identify account-related data associated with your Voira account unless we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

This may include:

  • account-related data associated with your email/user ID;
  • progress data associated with your account if stored;
  • saved vocabulary associated with your account if stored;
  • analysis history associated with your account if stored.

What May Remain

  • transaction or entitlement records required by Google Play, RevenueCat, or legal / accounting / security obligations;
  • anonymized or aggregated logs that no longer identify you;
  • data stored only on your device until you clear app data or uninstall Voira.

Subscription Note

Deleting your app account or app data does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop billing, cancel SpeakPlus from Google Play: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
